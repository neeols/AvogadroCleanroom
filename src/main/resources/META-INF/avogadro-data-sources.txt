1. NUCLEAR PROPERTIES & ISOTOPES: NUBASE2020

================================================================================



* Reference: F. G. Kondev, M. Wang, W. J. Huang, S. Naimi, and G. Audi,

  "The NUBASE2020 evaluation of nuclear physics properties",

  Chinese Physics C 45, 030001 (2021).

* DOI: https://doi.org/10.1088/1674-1137/abddae

* License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

* Published ASCII Table: https://amdc.impcas.ac.cn/masstables/Ame2020/nubase_4.mas20

  Pinned SHA-256: 1585a5eea86c5e17e90307c7e6e786d060049c4039e392a261ff6db977df9859

* Adaptation: Selected ground-state isotope records, normalized fields, and

  source flags.



Dataset Details:

----------------

* Contains 3,339 ground-state records for atomic numbers 1-118, including

  four-digit discovery years in columns 115-118 of that edition.

* Exclusions: Unobserved estimates, excited states, and isomeric states.

* Notation Flags:

  - '#' indicates an estimated mass or half-life (does not denote the absence

    of a nuclide).

  - 'stbl' denotes stable half-lives.

  - Includes historical tentative assignments from NUBASE2020 (not claims of

    discoveries confirmed through the current year).

2. REPRESENTATIVE ISOTOPES: CIAAW

================================================================================



* References:

  - Commission on Isotopic Abundances and Atomic Weights (CIAAW),

    Isotopic Compositions of the Elements 2024:

    https://www.ciaaw.org/isotopic-abundances.htm

  - Radioactive Elements Compilation (based on NUBASE2020, March 29, 2022):

    https://www.ciaaw.org/radioactive-elements.htm



Selection Guidelines:

---------------------

* Reference mass numbers are explicitly pinned for all 118 elements.

* Natural References: Utilize the greatest representative abundance where

  meaningful (e.g., Pb-208 is explicitly chosen due to natural lead abundance

  variations).

* Radioactive References: Selected from the CIAAW compilation; where multiple

  comparable candidates exist, the chosen value serves as a reference rather

  than a unique longest-lived isotope claim.

* Neutron Counts: Never derived from rounded atomic weights.

3. ELECTRON CONFIGURATIONS & ELEMENT CARDS

================================================================================



* Primary Sources: Transcribed from standard element references including

  Encyclopaedia Britannica and PubChem.

* Visual Presentation: Category colors follow the shared Avogadro element

  palette. All graphics and text are rendered natively by Avogadro; original

  reference images and article text are not bundled.



Specific Adjustments & Computations:

-----------------------------------

* Lawrencium (Z = 103): Configuration corrected to [Rn]5f14 7s2 7p1 using the

  NIST Elemental Data Index:

  https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/Elements/elInfo.pl?element=103

* Shell Populations: Neutral shell populations (including noble-gas cores) are

  derived directly from these configurations.

* Superheavy Elements: Configurations marked as predicted/calculated in

  element facts serve as schematic models rather than measured atomic shell

  images.
